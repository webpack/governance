# webpack Funding & Disbursement Policy

This policy governs the collection, approval, and disbursement of all funds held by or on behalf of the webpack project, which includes, but is not limited to:

- Open Collective (funds legally held by Open Source Collective, the project's fiscal host)

## Definitions

- **TSC**: the webpack Technical Steering Committee.
- **CPC**: the Cross Project Council of the OpenJS Foundation.
- **Fiscal host**: Open Source Collective, the legal entity holding funds on the project's behalf via the Open Collective platform.
- **Administrator**: a person holding administrator access to a fund account under this policy.
- **Personal interest**: a person holds a personal interest in an expense whenever the recipient being paid would benefit that person in any way, directly or indirectly. This includes, without limitation: being the recipient's employer, employee, or contractor (in either direction); being the recipient's business or romantic partner; sharing a household or immediate family with the recipient; or holding a financial stake in the recipient.
- **Independent approver**: an administrator who is neither the recipient of an expense nor holds any personal interest in it.
- **Regular recipient**: a person who has received project funds before and is expected to receive them again.

## Governing principle

**No person may approve, schedule, or release a payment of which they are the recipient or in which they hold a personal interest.** Every disbursement requires the approval of at least two independent approvers, and the expiry of the objection window described below without an unresolved objection.

## Affirmations

**By submitting an expense, a recipient swears** that they have read this policy as it stands on the day they submit and agree to be bound by that version; that everything stated in the expense is true and complete, and that the hours claimed were actually worked and the costs actually incurred by them; that compensation was agreed in advance as required below, and they have linked that agreement; that the expense has not been paid or reimbursed before, in whole or in part, by the project or anyone else; that they have disclosed anything that would give an administrator a personal interest in it; that they are not subject to sanctions that would make the payment unlawful; and that they understand a false statement here is a violation of this policy. The recipient agrees to repay anything paid to them in violation of this policy.

**By approving an expense, an approver swears** that they are an independent approver to that expense, that they have reviewed it against the version of this policy that governs it, and that they know of nothing that would make the recipient's statement untrue or make themselves biased in any way.

**By submitting, receiving, approving, or otherwise managing project funds, all those involved swear** that they are abiding by all laws applicable to them, including United States law applicable to the payment, and any laws binding on the OpenJS Foundation or the fiscal host.

## Administrative access

All TSC members shall hold administrator access to each and every fund account at all times. At least one administrator must not be regular recipients of project funds. Withholding or refusing administrator access from an eligible TSC member is itself a violation of this policy, except where access is suspended or revoked as an enforcement measure under the Enforcement section.

## Approval of disbursements

To receive a disbursement, a recipient shall submit each expense with a description, category, amount, the statements required above, along with supporting detail such as hours, tasks, or deliverables. Two independent approvers shall each review the expense for compliance with this policy, and allow for objects during the below mentioned period, before it is released.

The OpenJS Foundation, the webpack project, or the fiscal host may, and their sole discretion, and with legal counsel, require a recipient to complete, before any payment is made, any lawful verification deemed necessary. This includes, without limitation, identity verification, background checks, proof of residency or tax status, sanctions screening, and tax or banking documentation. Payment may be withheld until required verification is completed, but refusing verification is not a violation of this policy, and payees have the right to do so.

**Objection window.** No expense may be released until 72 business hours have passed from its submission being visible to all administrators. Any administrator may object within that window. A sustained objection blocks payment until it is resolved by a TSC vote, in which the any member holding a personal interest in the expense may not vote.

Where public disclosure of an expense would itself create risk (such as payment for security work that cannot be described publicly until a remedy is released) the description may be redacted to the minimum necessary. Even so, as much information as possible should still be recorded publicly with the appropiate redactions; the fact that an expense relates to security work is not itself damaging information.

## Permitted purposes

Funds may be disbursed only for the following purposes. Specific exceptions may be approved by a formal TSC vote, in which any member holding a personal interest in the exception may not vote:

- **Maintenance and development**: compensation for maintenance or development work agreed in advance.
- **Community engagement and management**: compensation for the fostering and supporting of the webpack community, including it's maintainers, contributors, and users.
- **Community and events**: travel, conference, and meetup costs approved in advance.

For any payments, regardless of task, **compensation must be agreed in advance.** An agreement counts as being made in advance only if it is recorded publicly, names the work and the rate or amount, and predates the work itself. Only work the TSC has agreed beforehand to compensate is eligible. Contributing without a prior agreement to be paid (by fixing issues, opening pull requests, or otherwise) creates no entitlement to compensation. **Contributions are an act of volunteering, and payment is not a right.**

## Rates and limits

No individual may receive more than USD 5,000 in any calendar month across all categories without a specific recorded TSC exception ratified via a vote, approved with interested members recused as described above.

Where total disbursements in a calendar month exceed one-twelfth of the project's available budget for the calendar year, no further expenses may be approved and the TSC must conduct a review before the next payment cycle, so as to prevent running the budget dry. Expenses already approved before the threshold was crossed shall still be paid.

## Enforcement

**Any failure to abide by this policy is a violation of it.**

A disbursement made in violation of this policy is subject to reversal, and the TSC will require repayment. An administrator responsible for a violation may have their access to Open Collective, or their status with the webpack project, suspended or revoked depending on the decision of OpenJS leadership (including the Technical Steering Committee, Cross Project Council, and Code of Conduct Committee).

## Amendment

Amendment of this policy, including any change to the PTS algorithm, requires a proposal and vote by the TSC, followed by ratification by the CPC.
