# Open Collective


## Intent

This documentation is intended to provide guidance on how funds from Open Collective is used in the organization the reasoning behind it.


## How Open Collective works

Open Collective is a platform that allows communities to raise and manage funds transparently. It provides a way for organizations, projects, and individuals to receive financial support from their community members, sponsors, and donors.

## Fund Usage

The fund raised through Open Collective is used to support the most essential parts of webpack, the bundler work itself. When changes are done in webpack/webpack, most of the time packages dependent on this need adjustments such as loaders and plugins that belong to the organization. In addition to that, on occasion, Pull Requests to core are eligible to be expensed to the webpack Open Collective as the developers in the core working group see fit. It is important that the funds available for the organization are rationally used to avoid that the organization runs out of funds and is unable to support the work that is being done.

## Calculating payouts

Previously the organization used a tool to calculate the payout if contributors can expense their work if a certain threshold is reached, but due decline in funding and abusing the system such as submitting bulk Pull Requests to any repository owned by webpack, it has been disabled. The organization is now using a fixed amount for each core members work that has been done since the creation of a webpack Open Collective. This amount has been set to `$7000` per month to a full time contributor. Previously this person was the creator of webpack, Tobias Koppers (@sokra), but since Tobias has left the project, the full time contributor is now Alexander Akait (@alexander-akait). Based on the work needed in core, a standard `$2000` is given to critical core contributors that applies core changes that provide substaintial value to the project. Presently, there are two core working group members that are eligible for this amount, and they are: Hai (@hai-x) and Xiao (@xiaoxiaojx). The organization is also open to other contributors that have been contributing to the project for a long time and have provided substantial value to the project. The core working group will evaluate these contributors on a case by case basis per [TSC guidelines](https://github.com/webpack/tsc/blob/main/FUNDING.md#transparency-and-reporting).

Instead of paying per hour, the core contributions often exceeds a full time job, contributions over multiple months and it is more rational to pay a fixed amount to contributors that have provided substantial value to the project. This ensures that contributors are compensated fairly for their work, while also allowing the organization to manage its finances effectively and intuitively.

## Prioritization of expenses

The organization will prioritize expenses based on the most critical needs of the webpack project. This includes funding for core contributors, essential changes related to the bundler. Communication work and community engagement is not considered a critical part of the project and will not be prioritized for funding, but will be considered on a case by case basis if there is leftover money after paying the most critical parts of webpack and the core working group. The organization will also consider requests for funding from contributors who have provided substantial value to the project, as long as it aligns with the organization's economy and priorities.


## Transparency and Reporting

The organization will provide regular updates on the financial status of the Open Collective, including income, expenses, and payouts to contributors. This ensures that all community members have visibility into how funds are being utilized and can hold the organization accountable for its financial decisions. A complete list of all expenses and payouts will be made available on the Open Collective platform, along with detailed explanations for each transaction. Each expense in Open Collective is reviewed by Alexander Akait (@alexander-akait) and Even Stensberg (@evenstensberg) to ensure that it aligns with the organization's goals and priorities, which is to keep webpack sustainable through careful financial management and contributions. Additionally, each expense is reviewed by an Open Collective employee to ensure that it meets the legal and community standards for transparency and accountability. The development and management of the funds are done by two organization owners one of which, requires to be a [webpack Technical Steering Committee](https://github.com/webpack/tsc) member, which is determined on seniority and experience in the project.


## Requesting payouts

If there is leftover money from paying the most critical part of webpack and the core working group, the organization is open to requests for payouts from contributors that have provided substantial value to the project. The core working group will notify the TSC and the TSC will evaluate these requests on a case by case basis per [TSC guidelines](https://github.com/webpack/tsc/blob/main/FUNDING.md#budgeting-principles). The vote requires a majority of the TSC members to approve the payout request. 


## Changes to this document

This document is subject to change as the organization sees fit, when finances requires to re-align, and will be updated when changes are made. For instance, if the organization sees that the current economics has a negative trend, it may be adjusted by reducing payouts to ensure that funds are used effectively and efficiently.
